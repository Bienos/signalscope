# Compliance and privacy

Prohibited: TikTok scraping, access-control bypass, proxy rotation, CAPTCHA solving, login farming, fingerprint manipulation, app emulation, video downloads, permanent thumbnails, face recognition, age estimation, follower-list collection, and commenter identities by default.

Use only an explicitly authorised/commercially licensed source. Store minimal metadata and official links. Default retention: username 12 months; display name/captions/hashtags 6 months; aggregate metrics/scores 24 months; authorised comments 30 days; authorised transcripts 90 days; temporary frames immediately deleted; full video never stored. Deletion removes active records/reports and retains a minimal reason/date audit record. Child-related material is flagged, never inferred using facial analysis, and apparent minors are not recommended commercially.

## How we use public social data

This internal service analyses licensed, provider-reported public-post metadata to identify patterns in a monitored sample. It does not claim complete TikTok coverage, host videos, identify children, or decide that accounts are fraudulent. Requests to remove a video or creator can be processed from Settings.
